FAQ

Why do I have to apply?

The group is about 20 people, and everyone in it should get as much from each other as from the visits. Your application also tells us what you're building, so we can match visits and supplier meetings to it.

Who are you looking for?

Founders choosing suppliers in the next six months, and investors active in robotics and hardware. We don't take defence companies.

What is the interview?

A 15-minute call about what you're building and what you want from the week. We decide within 2 days and let you know either way.

When do I pay?

Nothing when you apply. If you're offered a place, pay in full within 7 days, or pay 30% within 72 hours to hold it and the rest by 30 November. If fewer than 10 people have booked by 30 November, the trip doesn't run and everyone gets a full refund.

When should I book flights?

After we confirm the trip on 30 November. Fly into Shenzhen Bao'an (SZX) on 11 January, where we pick you up. If you fly into Hong Kong, we send directions for the border crossing.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. Many nationalities can enter China visa-free, and US passport holders may qualify for 240-hour visa-free transit if arriving from one country and leaving for another. Check the current rules for your passport. We provide invitation letters if you need a visa.

Is it only for engineers?

No. Founders and investors from every background join; you just need a reason to be in Shenzhen.

Will there be translation?

Yes. A bilingual coordinator travels with the group and translates in every meeting.

Can I bring samples or drawings?

Yes, and they make supplier meetings more useful. Tell us in advance so we can match suppliers.
