why shenzhen

what you can't see from home

Few places put so many engineers, component suppliers, prototyping shops, factories, researchers and investors within an hour's drive of each other. Being that close to suppliers is why hardware teams in Shenzhen iterate so quickly.

The week is built around the questions founders ask once they're on the ground: what worked for the companies we visit and what didn't, where the real bottlenecks are, which parts are turning into commodities, and where the next opportunities are.

What you get from the week

  • Warm introductions at around 15 factories and company headquarters, with quotes in hand and WeChat contacts you can follow up with.
  • The whole supply chain. In robotics, from reducers and motors to joints, limbs and full platforms. In consumer electronics, from PCBs and enclosures to molds, assembly, QA and packaging.
  • How the ecosystem works: how far factory automation has gone, how prototypes become mass production, and how China's startup and investor scene operates.
  • Better prices. Suppliers negotiate differently when you're standing in their factory.
  • Doing business in China: how to negotiate, how to build trust, and why relationships (guanxi) matter.
  • Local mixers with Shenzhen founders, engineers you could hire and potential partners.
  • A group worth staying in touch with: 25–30 founders, investors and operators across robotics, physical AI, consumer electronics, energy and neurotech.

By the end of the week you'll know the city well enough to come back on your own and go straight to your suppliers.

how it works

apply, talk to us, then we decide

bay^2 is by application. Not everyone who applies gets a place.

  1. Apply online

    It takes about five minutes. Applying is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

  2. Short interview call

    We read every application and invite applicants to a short call. We'll ask what you're building, what you want to leave Shenzhen with, and whether the dates and price work for you. It's also your chance to ask us anything.

  3. We make a decision

    We decide who joins based on your application and the call. We keep the group to 25–30 people and pick a mix of founders, investors and operators who will get value from each other. We'll tell you either way.

  4. Seats open 9 November, 9am PT

    Approved applicants get a booking link. Seats are confirmed only on payment and go in the order people book, so the earlier you book, the lower your price.

  5. 30 November: trip confirmed

    The trip runs once at least 10 people have booked. Wait until then before booking flights.

  6. 14 December: balance due

    Then you get a pre-trip pack and we hold a group call before we fly.

If fewer than 10 people have booked by 30 November 2026, we cancel the trip and refund everyone in full.

faq

questions

Why do I have to apply?

We keep the group to 25–30 people and choose a mix of founders, investors and operators who will get value from each other. Your application also tells us what you're building, so we can match visits and supplier meetings to it.

What happens on the call?

It's a short conversation about what you're building, what you want from the week and whether the dates and price work for you. We decide on your place after the call, based on your application and what we talked about.

When do I pay?

Nothing is due when you apply. If you're approved, you get a booking link when seats open on 9 November at 9am PT. Your seat is confirmed when you pay, and the balance is due on 14 December. If fewer than 10 people have booked by 30 November, you get a full refund.

Which companies will we visit?

The itinerary lists the hosts we expect to visit, plus others we're considering. We confirm each one in writing in November and send you the final list before the balance is due. We may add hosts beyond those listed, chosen around what the group is building.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. Many nationalities can currently enter China without a visa, and US passport holders may qualify for the 240-hour visa-free transit scheme if they arrive from one country and leave for a different one. The rules change, so check the latest requirements for your passport before you book flights. We provide invitation letters for anyone who needs a visa.

When should I book flights?

After we confirm the trip on 30 November. Arrive at Shenzhen Bao'an Airport (SZX) on 11 January and leave on 17 January.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Founders, investors and operators all join. What matters is a real interest in how hardware gets made.

Will there be translation?

Yes. A bilingual coordinator travels with the group and translates in every meeting.

Can I bring samples or drawings?

Yes. Drawings, a bill of materials or a sample make supplier meetings far more useful. Tell us in advance so we can match suppliers, and keep anything confidential under an NDA.
