Why Shenzhen

Almost everything a robot is made of is designed, built or sold within an hour of Shenzhen. This page is the case for going now, and the week in detail.

Three reasons

  1. The whole stack, one drive apart

    Reducers, motors, joints, hands, sensors, boards and finished robots are made in the same region. In one week you can follow a robot from its parts to the company selling it.

  2. Hardware at software speed

    Parts off the shelf the same day in Huaqiangbei, circuit boards back in a day or two, moulding and machining down the road. Seeing how fast the loop runs changes how you plan your own.

  3. Access goes to people who show up

    Good factories are rarely idle, so they serve the customers they know first. Founders who have met a supplier in person, over tea and a factory walk, get answered faster than an email from abroad.

The week in depth

Provisional. Hosts are confirmed in writing in November. Each visit is a guided tour, a talk from the team and Q&A, translated by our bilingual coordinator.

  1. mon 11 jan

    Arrival

    Pickup at Shenzhen Bao'an airport and check-in at the hotel in Nanshan.

    • Opening session: who's in the group and what each person wants from the week
    • Welcome dinner
  2. tue 12 jan

    Humanoids

    Three humanoid makers in Nanshan, each with a showroom, live demos and time with the team.

    • Full-size humanoids and their deployments in factories and public services
    • Bipedal and wheel-legged platforms, demonstrated live
    • How these companies went from lab to thousands of orders
  3. wed 13 jan

    Inside the robot

    The parts that make humanoids work.

    • Tactile sensors and dexterous hands
    • A showroom of joints, reducers and hands from Shenzhen's robot-parts cluster
    • A non-invasive brain-computer interface lab
    • Evening meetup with local hardware founders
  4. thu 14 jan

    Dongguan

    An hour north, where volume manufacturing happens.

    • A robotics incubator at Songshan Lake, where several well-known hardware companies began
    • Working factory floors: electronics assembly and SMT lines
    • Makers of motors, moulded parts and cable harnesses
  5. fri 15 jan

    The low-altitude economy

    Shenzhen's streets and skies as a test bed.

    • A drone delivery base and its flight-control room
    • A ride in a driverless robotaxi
    • A carmaker that builds its own batteries, chips and motors, and its sky-train
    • Dinner with local founders and investors
  6. sat 16 jan

    Market day

    The Huaqiangbei electronics market and Shenzhen's hardware stores.

    • Floors of components, modules, tools and repair benches
    • Hardware flagship stores for drones, 3D printers and new devices
    • Bring your parts list: where there's a fit, we add a supplier meeting
    • Closing dinner
  7. sun 17 jan

    Departure

    Airport transfers. Hong Kong directions on request.

Optional evenings: Huaqiangbei after dark, a Chinese bathhouse, karaoke.
