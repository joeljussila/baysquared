Privacy

last updated 12 october 2026

delta & bay organises a trip to Shenzhen for founders, investors and operators. This page explains what we collect when you apply, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

We collect the information you submit through the application form: your name, email, company, where you're based, your LinkedIn profile, your X profile and personal website if you add them, your role, the area you work in, what you're building, what you want from the trip, whether you can make the dates and, if you tell us, how you heard about us. We also record when you applied and your application's status, and receive information you send us when you contact us.

We haven't added advertising trackers or analytics tools to this site. Our hosting, database and font providers receive technical information, such as your IP address and request details, when your browser connects to their services.

Why we use it

  • To review your application and decide who joins the trip.
  • To contact you about your application, the interview call and booking.
  • To plan visits and supplier meetings around what you're building.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. We won't share what you're building with host companies or suppliers without asking you first.

Where it's kept

Applications are stored in a database run by Supabase, hosted in the United States. Access is restricted to authorised members of the delta & bay team and service providers involved in operating and supporting these services. The site is hosted by Vercel, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

We use Resend to email internal notifications about new applications to our team. These notifications contain an application reference and a link to Supabase, rather than your application answers.

How long we keep it

We keep applications while we plan and run the trip, and delete them within 12 months after it ends. If you'd like to hear about future trips, we'll ask before keeping your details longer.

Your choices

To request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, email info@deltaandbay.com. We handle requests within the time limits required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions, which we'll explain if they apply.
